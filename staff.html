🌴 Holidays of the business

On these days nobody has to come: the staff portals stay closed, nobody is marked absent and the day is paid. Each person’s weekly off is set in Staff → Edit. A single person’s leave: Month → tap the day → L.

⏰ Late

Staff with a login check in with one tap when they open AuraScale. A working day with no check-in becomes Absent by itself after midnight.

📲 Staff without a login (optional: attendance by WhatsApp)

For a helper or delivery boy who has no AuraScale login: print this QR and keep it in the shop. They scan it → WhatsApp opens → Send. Their WhatsApp mobile must be saved in Staff. (Works after the WhatsApp setup in Make.)
