Invoices & Payments

AuraScale tax invoices to nursing homes. One is created automatically for every payment.

Record a UPI / cash payment you received

Plan payment: puts the business on that plan, moves the renewal date forward (1, 7 or 14 months with the launch offer), unlocks a locked account and issues one GST tax invoice for the amount. Top-up: adds 100 ID scans. Check the money has arrived in your UPI app first. Online Cashfree payments are recorded automatically.

Plans — price for each business

Choose each business’s plan (the price fills in from the price list; you can type a special price). Hospital Combo switches on Pharmacy (retail) and Lab. Complimentary = free: never locked and no subscription invoices. Wholesale is its own business: the owner adds it from “Add business” on the same login, then it shows here as linked.
